Marlowe's priorities and pipeline

What's on my plate: what's in flight, where I need help, what's queued next, and what I've shipped.

Status: On track = progressing as planned. Needs support = I need help from someone to keep moving. Awaiting review = waiting on feedback or approval. Blocked = cannot proceed.

Flags: Blocker, Risk, Direction change, Decision needed, Open question, Overdue. Everything except an open question comes with my recommendation.

Stages: Awaiting kickoff, Discovery, Iteration, Finalizing specs, Development, DQA, Complete.

Timeline: Bars run from start to design deliverable date (solid = active, dashed = planned). Diamonds are milestones (filled when done). A hatched line means handed off and with Eng; the bar is solid again when Design QA starts. A flag marks the launch date, and a dot marks a planned start where the design handoff is still TBD. Select a bar to jump to its card.

Updated: A small dot means this changed since the last Friday recap.

Colors: The dot on a pod label and the left edge of a card show the pod: Benefits / ME teal, Payer & Global orange, Insights blue, Channel Partners purple. Red and amber mean something needs attention, green means on track, and gray labels are just types.
